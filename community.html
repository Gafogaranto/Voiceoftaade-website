A thoughtful assistant is on the way.

The AI interface was not connected to an AI service in the earlier build, which is why you could not see it. This panel makes the feature visible, but responses are not enabled yet.

What must be connected before it can answer
  1. A secure server-side AI endpoint, so private API keys are never exposed in the browser.
  2. Clear permission rules for what community posts or private-room content the assistant may read.
  3. A visible AI label and safeguards against inventing quotations from your book.

No AI replies are simulated here. The assistant will only be enabled after its secure backend is configured and tested.
