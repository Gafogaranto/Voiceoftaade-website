OUR VALUES IN A WIDER WORLD

Stories with purpose.

Five global goals. Shared ideas. Explore the themes that inspire our publishing.

Explore all 17 UN goals ↗

Values-based editorial alignment; not UN affiliation or a claim of measured impact.

04
SDG 4 · EDUCATION

Quality Education

Encouraging reading, reflection and lifelong learning.

Read more about Goal 4 ＋

What the goal is about

Goal 4 calls for inclusive and equitable quality education and lifelong learning opportunities for everyone. Its scope includes literacy, relevant learning, skills development and access to educational opportunities.

How the theme connects here

Books, reflective writing and reader discussions can encourage curiosity, strengthen reading habits and make ideas easier to explore. These are educational contributions in spirit; they do not replace formal schooling or prove a measured education outcome.

Read the UN's Goal 4 targets ↗
03
SDG 3 · WELL-BEING

Good Health and Well-being

Reflecting on well-being, perspective and healthier relationships.

Read more about Goal 3 ＋

What the goal is about

Goal 3 seeks to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for people of all ages. It spans health services, prevention, mental health and well-being, and stronger capacity to respond to health risks.

How the theme connects here

Thoughtful writing can invite readers to reflect on balance, supportive relationships, personal perspective and the importance of seeking appropriate help. It is not diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for qualified health support.

Read the UN's Goal 3 targets ↗
08
SDG 8 · WORK & GROWTH

Decent Work and Economic Growth

Exploring discipline, purposeful ambition and responsible work.

Read more about Goal 8 ＋

What the goal is about

Goal 8 promotes sustained, inclusive and sustainable economic growth, productive employment and decent work for all. It also recognises entrepreneurship, creativity, innovation and safe, fair working conditions.

How the theme connects here

Publishing can explore the habits, ideas and decisions involved in learning, responsible ambition and meaningful work. These reflections support conversation; they are not a claim that the platform has created jobs or generated economic impact.

Read the UN's Goal 8 targets ↗
16
SDG 16 · PEACE & JUSTICE

Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Encouraging empathy, thoughtful dialogue and peaceful relationships.

Read more about Goal 16 ＋

What the goal is about

Goal 16 aims to promote peaceful and inclusive societies, access to justice for all, and effective, accountable and inclusive institutions. Its themes include safety, rights, justice, transparency and participation.

How the theme connects here

Stories and discussions can help readers examine empathy, respectful disagreement, accountability and peaceful ways to handle differences. This is a contribution to reflection and dialogue, not a claim to deliver justice services or institutional reform.

Read the UN's Goal 16 targets ↗
17
SDG 17 · COLLABORATION

Partnerships for the Goals

Connecting readers, writers and educators through shared ideas.

Read more about Goal 17 ＋

What the goal is about

Goal 17 focuses on strengthening the means to achieve sustainable development and revitalising global partnerships. It includes cooperation, knowledge-sharing, capacity-building, technology and resources.

How the theme connects here

A publishing community can create opportunities for writers, readers, educators and organisations to share knowledge and explore joint projects. Any future partnership or outcome will be described only when it has actually been established.
